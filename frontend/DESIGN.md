## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance consumer tech e-commerce, balancing industrial stealth with commercial vitality. Built for audiophiles, gamers, and smart device enthusiasts, the visual tone merges technical precision with approachable modern retail merchandising.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** structure with **High-Contrast Dark Tech Chrome**:
- **Contrast Architecture:** Deep matte carbon surfaces frame featured product showcases and promotional heroes, while pure white and light eggshell backdrops provide uncompromised optical clarity for catalog browsing, specs, and checkout flows.
- **Warm Precision Accents:** Electric amber and tech gold illuminate interactive pathways, active navigation states, ratings, and promotional tags without skewing into harsh utilitarian neon.
- **Editorial Technology:** Visuals celebrate industrial hardware design through tactile rendering, crisp silhouette isolation, and understated geometric facet backdrops.

## Colors

The palette leverages a bifurcated tonal system engineered for high-conversion consumer electronics merchandising.

- **Primary Accent (`#e59b22` / `#f59e0b`):** Warm Amber / Tech Gold. Reserved for high-value interactive focal points, add-to-cart CTAs, discount ribbons, active tab indicators, and 5-star customer rating stars.
- **Secondary Dark Chrome (`#1a1c20`, `#121316`, `#22252a`):** Deep Matte Carbon. Applied to top banners, featured category tiles, promo carousels, and footer shells. Provides theatrical contrast for hardware hardware photography.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#2563eb`):** Precision Signal Blue. Used sparingly for secondary status pills, auxiliary connectivity tags (e.g., Bluetooth, Wi-Fi), and verified tech spec badges.
- **Neutral Scale:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: Crisp Pure White (`#ffffff`) on catalog pages and item detail cards; Soft Studio Gray (`#f8f9fa`) for page foundations and input wrappers.
  - `Dividers & Hairlines`: Subtle Warm Slate (`#e5e7eb`).
  - `Typography`: Pitch Charcoal (`#111827`) for authoritative headlines and titles; Steel Gray (`#6b7280`) for meta text, item codes, and specifications.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes structural cadence, clear numerical values, and swift scanning across dense commerce layouts.

- **Type Scale Strategy:** Powered uniformly by **Inter** to ensure maximum digital rendering clarity across retina displays and varied viewport densities.
- **Editorial Badges & Category Trackers:** Labels (`label-lg`, `label-md`) adopt semi-bold, uppercase tracking (`0.05em` - `0.08em`) to demarcate hardware categories, subheadings (e.g., "SPECIAL DISCOUNT"), and micro-navigation anchors.
- **Pricing & Headings:** Product names use tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) with prominent font weights (600/700) to stand out sharply against both dark carbon cards and light retail grids.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid anchors the commerce layout with tailored vertical rhythms:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and a max-width container of `1320px`. Top hero sections span the full width or 12 columns; promotional editorial tiles divide cleanly into asymmetric modular clusters (e.g., 4-column feature banner alongside 4-column stacked quadrants); product grids default to 5-column or 4-column repeats.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters. Product grids automatically adjust to 3 columns, and asymmetric promo tiles convert into balanced 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Product grids collapse into a tight 2-column card catalog with edge-to-edge touch targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines physical surface planes with crisp optical boundaries:

- **Carbon Dark Depth:** Hero carousels and featured promo tiles operate on dark levels (`#1a1c20`). They incorporate subtle low-opacity geometric meshes or dark linear gradients (`linear-gradient(180deg, #22252a 0%, #16171a 100%)`) without heavy drop shadows, relying on contrast separation against light backgrounds.
- **Product Surfaces & Low-Contrast Outlines:** Product cards sit on pure white (`#ffffff`) or frosted canvas with `1px` subtle border strokes (`#f0f1f3`). Card hover states trigger an ambient shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.
- **Amber Glow & Floating Elements:** Floating tooltips, sticky navigation headers, and floating cart controls utilize subtle glassmorphism with an ambient edge: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`, and border `rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.8)`. Primary CTA buttons carry an active warm glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(229, 155, 34, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** corner rounding (`roundedness: 1`), conveying high-precision engineering and structural reliability.

- Standard buttons, category tiles, and text input boxes feature crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Medium promo panels, product cards, and modal dialogs use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Product status tags, percentage discount bubbles, and floating circular iconography (e.g., wishlist hearts, pagination arrows) utilize full circles or pill boundaries (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / View Offer):** High-saturation amber fill (`#e59b22`), deep carbon text (`#111827`) or pure white, font weight 600. Flat resting state with a warm gold glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(229, 155, 34, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Tech Action:** Deep carbon background (`#1a1c20`), white text, amber border accent on hover.
- **Ghost & Text Links:** Uppercase with bold letter-spacing (`label-md`), accompanied by an underline indicator in amber (`#e59b22`) for active or hover states.

### Badges & Promo Chips
- **Discount Tags:** Circular or soft pill badges in warm amber (`#e59b22`) with bold white text (e.g., `-20%`, `NEW`).
- **Feature Tabs:** Underline tab design (`Top Products: LATEST / BEST SELLER / FEATURED`) where the active tab displays a high-contrast charcoal text label with a 2px amber accent bar directly below.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean vertical stack consisting of:
  1. Top-right wishlist/action trigger.
  2. Isolated hardware photography on neutral background (`#ffffff` or `#f9fafb`).
  3. 5-star rating row filled with amber gold (`#e59b22`).
  4. Product title in two-line clamp (`#111827`, 14px font size).
  5. Current price highlighted in charcoal or amber, paired with a muted strikethrough price (`#9ca3af`).
- **Hover Interaction:** Subtle elevation lift (`-2px`) with an instant outline reveal.

### Service Value Ribbons
- Horizontal three- or four-column banner placed beneath hero showcases featuring minimal hairline line-art icons (Free Shipping, 24/7 Support, Money Back) paired with uppercase sub-headers and muted slate descriptions.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Low-profile input bars with `1px` border (`#e5e7eb`), soft eggshell background (`#f9fafb`), and focus transitions triggering a crisp 1px ring in primary amber (`#e59b22`).
